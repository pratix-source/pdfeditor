Private, Fast and Browser-Based PDF Editing

Pratix PDF Toolkit is a free, professional-grade PDF editor that runs entirely inside your web browser. Unlike traditional PDF editors or cloud services, Pratix never uploads your files to any server. Every operation — merging, splitting, rotating, reordering, redacting — happens locally on your device using modern browser APIs.

Why Client-Side PDF Processing Matters

When you upload a PDF to a cloud service, your sensitive documents travel across the internet and are stored on third-party servers. With Pratix PDF Toolkit, your files never leave your browser tab. This is especially important for legal documents, financial records, medical files, and any confidential business content.

What You Can Do

  • PDF Merge: Combine multiple PDF files into one document in any order you choose.
  • PDF Split: Extract a specific page range from a PDF into a new file.
  • PDF Rotate: Rotate individual pages or all pages by 90°, 180°, or 270°.
  • Page Delete: Remove unwanted pages visually and download the cleaned PDF.
  • Page Reorder: Drag and drop PDF pages into any order you need.
  • Images to PDF: Convert JPG and PNG images into a single PDF document.
  • Metadata Cleaner: Strip author, title, keywords, and creator metadata from PDF files.
  • PDF Viewer: View any PDF with zoom, page navigation, and thumbnails.
  • Visual Redaction: Draw black rectangles over sensitive areas and permanently embed them into the PDF.

What Is Not Included — and Why

Some PDF operations are intentionally excluded from this toolkit because they cannot be performed reliably or safely in a browser environment:

  • OCR (Optical Character Recognition): Requires heavy ML models that are impractical to run client-side at production quality.
  • Password Remove / Crack: Removing encryption from a PDF you do not own is a legal and ethical issue. We do not support this.
  • Office Conversion (Word/Excel/PowerPoint): These formats require complex server-side rendering pipelines that cannot be replicated in a browser.
  • Server-Side Compression: Deep PDF compression (e.g. re-encoding fonts and images) requires server processing. Our toolkit offers basic save optimization only.
